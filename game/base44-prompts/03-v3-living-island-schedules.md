# Pocket Island v3: a living island (scheduled content) and 6 new adventures

Update the existing **Pocket Island** app. Keep all v1 and v2 features.

## Goal of this version
Make the island feel alive between visits, driven by scheduled tasks, so that coming back is a pleasant surprise and never an obligation.

## 1. Nightly "island night" task (scheduled task, every day at 03:00 UTC)
- For every island (cloud-saved ones on the server; local-only ones by computing the same result from the last-visit timestamp when the app opens):
  - pick 1 gentle change from a pool: a flower blooms, a tree grows a bit, a small house gets a new window, a rainbow appears, a creature builds a sandcastle.
  - store it as a "while you were away" note.
- Changes only **add** things. Nothing decays, breaks, gets dirty or disappears.
- Cap it: at most 3 new changes are shown on return, however long the child was away, so a long break never feels like "missing out".

## 2. "While you were away" moment
- When the child opens the game, show a short animated tour (skippable) of what changed: "Look! The sunflower bloomed!"
- Then drop the child straight onto the island.

## 3. Monthly visitor (scheduled task, 1st of every month)
- A new friendly visitor creature arrives on every island with a small story and one adventure attached.
- The visitor **stays forever**. There is no deadline and no "leaves in 3 days". Past visitors remain available to everyone.
- In the parent zone, show a list of upcoming visitors so parents know what's coming.

## 4. Six new adventures (2 per age level)
- Little (4-6): "The Upside-Down Garden" (sort by shape, no reading) and "Bath Time for the Hippo" (count bubbles 1-10 with narration).
- Middle (7-9): "The Lighthouse Code" (simple patterns and sequences) and "Lost Letters" (help the postman match addresses, light reading).
- Big (10-12): "The Bridge Engineers" (build a bridge that holds weight, logic and planning) and "Mystery of the Missing Moon" (a detective story with clues and deduction, several possible answers).
- Each adventure: 3 to 5 minutes, funny ending, calm stopping point, gentle hints, no fail screen.

## 5. Parent email addition
- Add the "while you were away" changes and new visitors to the Sunday digest (still only if the child played that week).

## Hard rules (unchanged, still apply to everything in this version)
- No ads. No store, prices or "buy" buttons anywhere in the child's area.
- No virtual currency, loot boxes, surprise boxes or paid random rewards.
- No streaks, "you missed a day", countdown timers or limited-time offers.
- No chat, no contact between users, no friends list.
- No creature is ever sad, hungry or hurt because the child was away. Nothing is ever lost.
- A visible exit button on every screen; the child can stop at any moment without losing anything.
- The child never signs up and never types an email, a real name, a location or a photo.
- Emails go **only to the parent**, only after the parent opts in, always with a one-tap unsubscribe link, and never contain marketing, urgency or guilt ("your child misses the island" is forbidden).
- Scheduled content never creates urgency: no expiry, no "only today", no counters.

## Done when
- Opening the game after 1 day shows 1 change, after 10 days shows no more than 3, and nothing was removed.
- On the 1st of the month a new visitor appears with a playable adventure and is still there next month.
- All 6 new adventures are playable on a phone in portrait mode.
