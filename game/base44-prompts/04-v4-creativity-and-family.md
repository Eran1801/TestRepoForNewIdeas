# Pocket Island v4: creativity tools and family sharing

Update the existing **Pocket Island** app. Keep all v1 to v3 features.

## Goal of this version
Put more of the fun into making things. Children create, and only their own family sees it.

## 1. Adventure Builder (Middle and Big modes)
- The child can build their own mini adventure: pick a place on the island, a character, a problem ("the cat is stuck on the roof"), and 3 steps using ready-made puzzle blocks (find, match, sort, choose a path, choose an answer).
- Simple drag-and-drop, with a "Try it!" button to play it right away.
- Big mode adds: branching choices (two endings), a custom title and a short description.
- Saved adventures appear on the island as a signpost the child can tap to replay.

## 2. Sticker book and music maker
- **Sticker book:** every creature the child designs and every adventure they finish adds a sticker to a book. Stickers are earned by playing and creating, never bought, never random.
- **Music maker:** a simple 8-step beat grid where each creature is an instrument (the frog croaks, the bird tweets). The child can save a tune as the island's background music.

## 3. Family gallery (parent-approved only)
- With a parent account, the parent can add up to 4 family members (e.g. grandparents) by email in the parent zone.
- The child taps "Show my family" on a creation → it goes to a **parent approval queue** (not straight out).
- The parent approves in the parent zone → the family members get an email with the image or a link to play the child's adventure (read-only, no login needed, no comments, no likes).
- No public sharing, no strangers, no likes or view counts.

## 4. Coloring pages by email
- In the parent zone: "Email me coloring pages" turns any creature or island scene the child made into a black-and-white printable PDF and sends it to the parent's email.
- Optional scheduled task: on the 15th of every month, send opted-in parents one printable coloring page made from their child's creations of that month (only if they played that month).

## Hard rules (unchanged, still apply to everything in this version)
- No ads. No store, prices or "buy" buttons anywhere in the child's area.
- No virtual currency, loot boxes, surprise boxes or paid random rewards.
- No streaks, "you missed a day", countdown timers or limited-time offers.
- No chat, no contact between users, no friends list.
- No creature is ever sad, hungry or hurt because the child was away. Nothing is ever lost.
- A visible exit button on every screen; the child can stop at any moment without losing anything.
- The child never signs up and never types an email, a real name, a location or a photo.
- Emails go **only to the parent**, only after the parent opts in, always with a one-tap unsubscribe link, and never contain marketing, urgency or guilt ("your child misses the island" is forbidden).
- Nothing the child makes leaves the family, and nothing leaves without the parent approving it.

## Done when
- A 7 year old can build and play a 3-step adventure without help from an adult.
- A creation shared by the child reaches the grandparent's email only after the parent approves it.
- The coloring page PDF prints well on A4 and Letter.
