# Pocket Island v5: payments, family plan and child profiles

Update the existing **Pocket Island** app. Keep all v1 to v4 features.

## Goal of this version
Turn on real revenue in a way parents trust: one clear purchase or one family plan, bought only by the parent.

## 1. What is free and what is paid
- **Free forever:** the starting beach area, the creature creator (basic parts), 2 adventures per age level, the explore mode.
- **Pocket Island Full (one-time purchase):** the whole island, all current adventures, the Adventure Builder, the music maker, all creature parts.
- **Family Plan (monthly or yearly subscription):** everything in Full, plus the monthly visitor and new adventures every month, up to 4 child profiles, and cloud backup.
- Show exact prices in real currency. No coins, gems or bundles.

## 2. Where purchasing happens
- **Only** in the parent zone, behind the parent gate, with a parent account.
- When the child taps a locked area, show a friendly screen with no price and no buy button: "This part of the island is still being built. Ask a grown-up to open it for you." plus a "Back to the island" button.
- No purchase reminders, no discounts with a deadline, no "last chance".

## 3. Child profiles (Family Plan)
- The parent creates up to 4 profiles: a nickname (not a real name) and a creature avatar chosen from a set.
- Each profile has its own island, age level and progress.
- The child picks their avatar on the start screen. No passwords for children.

## 4. Emails for payments
- Send a receipt email after every purchase or renewal.
- **Scheduled task:** 3 days before a subscription renews, email the parent a plain reminder with the date, the amount and a one-tap "Manage or cancel" link.
- Cancelling is as easy as subscribing: one screen in the parent zone, no "are you sure" guilt screens. After cancelling, the child **keeps everything already created and unlocked content stays playable until the end of the period**. Creations are never deleted.

## 5. Parent zone page: "How we make money"
- A short, honest page: "We make money only from parents buying the game or the family plan. We never show ads, never sell data and never let children buy anything."

## Hard rules (unchanged, still apply to everything in this version)
- No ads. No store, prices or "buy" buttons anywhere in the child's area.
- No virtual currency, loot boxes, surprise boxes or paid random rewards.
- No streaks, "you missed a day", countdown timers or limited-time offers.
- No chat, no contact between users, no friends list.
- No creature is ever sad, hungry or hurt because the child was away. Nothing is ever lost.
- A visible exit button on every screen; the child can stop at any moment without losing anything.
- The child never signs up and never types an email, a real name, a location or a photo.
- Emails go **only to the parent**, only after the parent opts in, always with a one-tap unsubscribe link, and never contain marketing, urgency or guilt ("your child misses the island" is forbidden).

## Done when
- A child can never reach a price or a payment screen, even by tapping every locked thing.
- A parent can buy, get a receipt, get the reminder 3 days before renewal and cancel in under 30 seconds.
- After cancelling, the child's creatures and island are all still there.
